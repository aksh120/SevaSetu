SevaSetu is an institutional compliance copilot for first-time NGO founders in India. It guides them through legal structure formation (Trust, Society, Section 8) and the dependent statutory chain: PAN, 12A tax exemption, 80G donor deductions, NGO Darpan, CSR-1, and FCRA.

Today, founders navigate five disconnected portals with hidden prerequisite orders. Filing out of sequence wastes months, while middlemen charge Rs 2,000 to Rs 15,000 per step. Crucially, until registered, donations land in personal accounts, taxed as personal income. Two unremovable bottlenecks—in-person Sub-Registrar deed execution and the mandatory SBI New Delhi Main Branch FCRA account—we prepare founders to clear in one clean attempt.

SevaSetu replaces this friction with an authentic e-Pramaan SSO portal (Aadhaar OTP, PAN/DIN, DigiLocker), a 60-second branching intake, and plain-language guided modules featuring 1-click DigiLocker document verification. Submissions trigger live status progression on an adaptive suspension bridge dashboard. A built-in AI Notice Translator, running server-side with contractual zero data retention, breaks down scary Income Tax letters into plain actions and deadlines. Finally, our Verified Pro-Bono CA Network enables 1-click SHA-256 encrypted dispatch to empanelled ICAI partner firms for digital signature certification and audit review.

The full journey works end-to-end with 100% English/Hindi parity, WCAG AA dark/light themes, and offline print exports. What's mocked: government submissions and portal approval timers, clearly tagged with [Mocked] badges. At scale, this becomes a national document vault and automated compliance calendar.
